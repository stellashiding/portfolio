About Shi Ding

Shi Ding is an award-winning researcher at the Georgia Institute of Technology, where she explores how interactive agentic AI systems can enhance human learning, strengthen human agency, and align with human and social values. She is a Ph.D. Candidate in Digital Media at the Expressive Machinery Lab, advised by Regents’ Professor Brian Magerko. Her research broadly focuses on human–AI interaction, human-centered AI evaluation, and accessible learning technologies.

Her work has been recognized with a Best Paper Honorable Mention (WIP) at ACM IDC 2026 and the CRIDC Research Award. She has also received an AI Child Safety Lab Fellowship. Her publications have appeared at ACM IDC, SIGCSE, ASSETS, and AIED, with additional first-author papers at NeurIPS and ACL workshops.

Beyond her core research, she has collaborated with faculty across disciplines at Georgia Tech, including Mark Riedl, Richmond Wong, Chris MacLellan, Michael Nitsche, and Jason Freeman.

She has presented her research at Harvard University, Delft University of Technology, UC Davis, UC Irvine, and the Georgia Tech Responsible Computing, AI, & Society Summit.

She has been an Area Chair for ACM DIS, a Session Chair for ACL, and a reviewer for conferences including CHI, DIS, FAccT, AMIA, C&C, NeurIPS, SIGCSE TS, and CogSci. She is also a reviewer for MIT Solve’s 2027 Global Challenges, evaluating technology-based solutions for social impact.

In industry, she previously worked as a Research Scientist at the AI startup Grasply AI. She is also a CREATE-X Fellow and the co-founder of Deeproject.AI, supported by the Quadrant-i Startup Grant, with mentorship from Richard Gruber and William Shawn Yeager.
