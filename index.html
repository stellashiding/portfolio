About

Hello! I'm Shi (Stella) Ding, a Ph.D. candidate at the Georgia Institute of Technology. My research focuses on human–AI alignment in real-world systems. I study how AI systems behave in practice and develop approaches to better understand, assess, and improve their performance in complex, real-world interactions. I work within the NSF-funded EarSketch project, a large-scale learning platform used by over 1.5 million learners, where I design and evaluate AI-powered systems in authentic educational settings. My work spans machine learning, human–computer interaction, and real-world AI deployment, with a focus on accessibility at scale. Previously, I worked as a research scientist at a U.S.-based AI startup, applying human-centered AI research to product development. I am broadly interested in building reliable and effective AI systems for real-world use.
