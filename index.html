About

Shi Ding is an award-winning researcher at the Georgia Institute of Technology, where she explores how frontier AI and interactive agentic systems can enhance human learning, strengthen human agency, and align with human and social values. She is a Ph.D. Candidate in Digital Media at the Expressive Machinery Lab, advised by Regents’ Professor Brian Magerko. Her research broadly focuses on human–AI interaction, human-centered AI evaluation, and accessible learning technologies.

Her honors include a Best Paper Honorable Mention (WIP) at ACM IDC 2026, the CRIDC Research Award, and an AI Child Safety Lab Fellowship. Her publications cover human–AI interaction, human-centered AI, accessible computing, and AI literacy, with work appearing at ACM IDC, SIGCSE, ASSETS, and AIED, alongside first-author papers at NeurIPS and ACL workshops.

Her research has been cited in CHI, DIS, and ASSETS papers,in Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence, and in work coauthored by researchers at Google Research, Google DeepMind, and Harvard Business School.

Beyond her core research, she has collaborated with faculty across disciplines at Georgia Tech, including Mark Riedl, Richmond Wong, Chris MacLellan, Michael Nitsche, and Jason Freeman.

She has presented her research at Harvard University, Delft University of Technology, UC Davis, UC Irvine, and the Georgia Tech Responsible Computing, AI, & Society Summit.

She has served as an Area Chair for ACM DIS, a Session Chair for ACL, and a reviewer for conferences including CHI, DIS, FAccT, AMIA, C&C, NeurIPS, SIGCSE TS, and CogSci. She will also serve as a reviewer for MIT Solve’s 2027 Global Challenges, evaluating technology-based solutions for social impact.

In industry, she previously worked as a Research Scientist at the AI startup Grasply AI. She is also a CREATE-X Fellow and the co-founder of Deeproject.AI, supported by the Quadrant-i Startup Grant, with mentorship from Richard Gruber and William Shawn Yeager.
