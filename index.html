About

I am a Ph.D. Candidate at the Georgia Institute of Technology, advised by Regents Professor Brian Magerko in the Expressive Machinery Lab.

My research lies at the intersection of Human–AI Interaction, AI Evaluation, and AI Alignment. I develop human-centered methods for understanding and evaluating how AI systems behave in real-world interactions, with a particular focus on large language models, AI agents, and human-facing AI systems.

At Georgia Tech, I contribute to the NSF-funded EarSketch project, where I study AI-supported learning, accessibility, and the design of AI systems that better support diverse learners. More broadly, I am interested in how AI systems reason, communicate, and collaborate with people in real-world contexts.
