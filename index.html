About

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Georgia Institute of Technology working on human–AI interaction, LLM evaluation, and alignment in real-world AI systems.

My research studies how AI systems behave across multi-turn interactions and develops structured approaches for evaluating reliability, transparency, and human-centered performance.

I work within the NSF-funded EarSketch project and co-founded Deeproject AI, focusing on evaluation and alignment for human-facing AI systems.
