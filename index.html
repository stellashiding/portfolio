About

I am a Ph.D. Candidate at the Georgia Institute of Technology, advised by Regents Professor Brian Magerko in the Expressive Machinery Lab.

My research lies at the intersection of human-centered AI, agentic AI, and AI evaluation. My goal is to make AI systems more reliable, transparent, and aligned with human needs.

At Georgia Tech, I design, develop, and evaluate human-facing AI systems through real-world deployments, including the NSF-funded EarSketch project , where I investigate human–AI interaction, LLM-powered learning, and accessibility in educational contexts.
