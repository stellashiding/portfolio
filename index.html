About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Digital Media at the Georgia Institute of Technology, advised by Regents' Professor Brian Magerko in the Expressive Machinery Lab.

My research focuses on human–AI interaction, LLM evaluation, and alignment in real-world AI systems. I study how AI systems behave across multi-turn interactions and develop structured evaluation approaches for understanding reliability, transparency, and human-centered performance.

I work within the NSF-funded EarSketch project, a large-scale creative coding platform used by over 1.5 million learners, where I design and evaluate AI-supported educational systems in authentic learning environments.

Previously, I worked as a research scientist at a U.S.-based AI startup, applying human-centered AI research to real-world product development.
