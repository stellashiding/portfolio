About

Hello! I'm Shi Ding (Stella)! I am a PhD student at the Expressive Machinery Lab at Georgia Institute of Technology, advised by Professor Brian Magerko, where I work on the NSF-funded EarSketch research project—a large-scale learning platform used by over 1.5 million learners. My research focuses on human-centered AI, AI-supported learning systems, and accessibility-driven design, examining how AI systems can be designed and evaluated to better support humans in real-world contexts. During my PhD, I also interned as a cognitive scientist at a U.S.-based AI startup, where I applied cognitive science and human–AI interaction research to product development. Before joining Georgia Tech, I was an educator (K–12 and higher education) and instructional designer; this experience continues to shape my research by connecting system design and evaluation with classroom-based practice. Outside of research, I enjoy playing tennis and badminton, as well as hiking.
