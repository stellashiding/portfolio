About

Hello! I'm Shi (Stella) Ding, a Ph.D. student at the Georgia Institute of Technology. My research focuses on human–AI alignment in real-world systems. I study how AI systems behave in practice and develop approaches to better understand, assess, and improve their performance in complex, real-world interactions. I work within the NSF-funded EarSketch project, a large-scale learning platform used by over 1.5 million learners, where I design and evaluate AI-powered systems that support code interaction and learning workflows in authentic educational settings. My work spans machine learning and human–computer interaction, with a focus on human–AI alignment and accessible learning systems. Previously, I worked as a research scientist at a U.S.-based AI startup, applying human-centered AI research to product development.
